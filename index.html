Our Conditions of Trade, in plain terms

Fee: a one-off lump sum per person placed — $3,000 + GST for under 4 hires, $2,500 + GST for 5–10, $2,000 + GST for 10–100. Invoiced once your candidate accepts the role and lodges their visa application.
Payment terms: due as a lump sum within 7 days of the invoice date.
Replacement guarantee: if a placed candidate is dismissed for underperformance or leaves within 3 months, we source a similar or better replacement within 3 months at no extra fee.
Refunds: available if your placed employee abandons the role for 3+ consecutive days within their first 90 days without notice. Not available if you can't provide a suitable workplace or wage, or the employee leaves due to injury, illness, death or force majeure.
What we do: we shortlist candidates matched to your job description and arrange online interviews within 14 days of your order. You then have 90 days to decide whether to accept a candidate and agree their hourly rate.
What you need ready: Employer Accreditation and a Job Check completed before offering the role, and a signed employment contract once a candidate is accepted.

Compliance autopilot
